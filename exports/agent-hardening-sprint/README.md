# Launch brief: Agent Hardening Sprint

> Fixed-fee red team of a small company's coding agents, MCP servers and CI agent jobs, then hardening through vendor-native managed settings, sandboxing and tested guardrails.

- Status: **unvalidated** — panel verdict PROMISING (mean 5.75, founder fit 6); not yet tested with real buyers.
- Riskiest assumption: Enough 15-150 person SaaS companies have a concrete trigger (a customer questionnaire item, auditor question or near-miss about coding agents) to pay about $6,500 for a red team and hardening now, directly or through a partner firm.
- First test: In two weeks: interview 10 SOC 2 readiness, vCISO or small pentest firms about how often clients get coding-agent or MCP questions and offer them the wholesale sprint; contact 20 CTOs from the founder's network and research readers, ask whether they have such a request in writing, and offer a pre-sold sprint with a $1,000 deposit.
- Success signal: At least 3 of 10 partner firms report clients with written coding-agent questions and 2 agree in writing to refer or resell, and at least 1 CTO pays a deposit at $6,500 (or 2 at the one-repository CI test).
- Open fatal/major objections: 18; claims never to reuse: 1
