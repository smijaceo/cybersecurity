# Launch brief: Safeguards Desk

> Verified FTC Safeguards program for small tax, mortgage and accounting offices: read-only Microsoft 365 checks, required scans and tests, and evidence from real configuration.

- Status: **unvalidated** — panel verdict PROMISING (mean 5.62, founder fit 7); not yet tested with real buyers.
- Riskiest assumption: Small financial offices above 5,000 consumers, or MSPs on their behalf, will pay about $2k/year for verified configuration and 314.4(d)(2) testing on top of the WISP and cyber insurance they already buy.
- First test: In two weeks, without building software: offer 15 MSPs that serve accounting or mortgage offices a white-label pilot, and email 40 mortgage brokers and established tax offices a fixed-fee $490 'Safeguards testing check' (manual external scan plus a read-only M365 review delivered by hand). In 10 owner calls, ask about consumer count, insurer questionnaires and whether anyone has asked for proof of testing.
- Success signal: At least 3 paid $490 checks or 1 MSP letter of intent covering 10+ client offices, and at least 4 of 10 owners above 5,000 consumers saying they have no annual test or vulnerability assessment and would pay to have one.
